About MoodBrew

MoodBrew is a hackathon frontend prototype built with HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript.

Recommendations come from transparent, rule-based scoring. They are not a real AI model, and we make no claim that any drink changes your emotions.

Nutrition values are estimates from sample per-serving data in script.js. Orders, reservations, ratings and seat availability are simulated and saved only in your browser.

MoodBrew · Frontend demo prototype · No real orders, payments or live seat data
